Delete your AIWA account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open AIWA and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is closed immediately and you are signed out on every device.

Without the app

Message AIWA support from the phone number or email on your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm it is you, then delete it within 7 days.

Contact AIWA support through the app's "Talk to AIWA" screen.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone number, email, profile photo and saved device token.
  • Your saved addresses and their map locations, and your trusted contacts.
  • For providers: your ID card photos and selfie, your gallery photos, and your public listing.
  • Any upcoming bookings are cancelled and the other party is told.

What is kept, and why

  • Past bookings, reviews, chat messages and payment records stay, with your name replaced by "Deleted user", so the other party's history, disputes and commission records still add up.
  • Safety (SOS) records and the admin audit log stay for safety and legal reasons.
  • Backups are overwritten within 14 days.
