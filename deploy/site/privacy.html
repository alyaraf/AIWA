Who we share it with

  • The other party of a booking: the provider sees your name, the service and, for home visits, your address. Each side sees the other's phone number only while a booking is confirmed and under way (for the call button); otherwise you talk through in-app chat.
  • Service providers that run AIWA for us: the SMS gateway [name], the email service [name], Google Firebase (push notifications), Sentry (crash reports) and our hosting provider [name, country]. They process data only on our instructions.
  • Your trusted contacts, only when you raise an SOS.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or someone's safety is at risk.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. When you delete it, we erase your name, phone, email, photo, saved addresses and trusted contacts, and for providers the ID photos, selfie and gallery. Past bookings, reviews, chats, payment records, safety records and the admin audit log are kept without your name, for disputes, accounting and safety. Backups on our server are overwritten within 14 days and off-site backup copies within 30 days. How to delete your account.

Your rights

You can see and correct your details in the app, ask for a copy of your data, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent (for example to location or notifications, in your phone's settings), and delete your account. Contact [privacy email]. You may also complain to Egypt's Personal Data Protection Centre.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. ID documents are stored outside the public web folder and served only to signed-in verification staff. Staff access is limited by role and logged.

Children

AIWA is not for people under [18]. We delete accounts we learn belong to a child.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app tells you before the change applies.
